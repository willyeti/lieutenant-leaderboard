How to read this board

  • The backbone is Arms Warrior (#4) and Arcane Mage (#5) — ~65k priority DPS over 131 and 110 logs. They rank "only" 4th–5th because a handful of small-sample specs edge them on a few hot games. For a spec that reliably melts lieutenants, these two are the answer.
  • The small-sample top is a caution, not a verdict. Assassination Rogue (#1, 8 logs) leapt from 67k to 83k just by adding four games; Feral (#2) and Shadow (#7) rank on 3 logs each. Rogues are genuinely strong single-target funnels — but the order up top would reshuffle with more data.
  • Established mid-pack: Havoc DH (#8, 16 logs) and Ret Paladin (#9, 19 logs) are trustworthy at ~58–60k. Elemental Shaman (#16, 76 logs) is a well-sampled but clearly lower-priority spec (~40k).
  • The cleave specs sit at the bottom: BM Hunter funnels the hardest of anyone (34% focus) but does the least raw damage (23.5k), and Balance Druid lands low too — the AoE-lean profiles that spread damage instead of funneling it.
  • Only 19 of ~26 DPS specs made the board. Fire/Frost Mage, MM/Survival Hunter, the Evoker specs, and two Warlock specs never hit 3 appearances — they're that rare in top +15–18 comps.
