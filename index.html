How to read this board

  • The reliable elite: Assassination Rogue, Arcane Mage, Arms Warrior, Retribution Paladin. All four sit at ~63–67k on 20–143 logs — statistically separated from the pack and trustworthy. If you want a spec that melts lieutenants, it's one of these.
  • Ret Paladin is the riser. In the elite-only cut it looked mid-pack on thin data; across the top 30% (33 logs) it's a clear top-4 priority-damage spec.
  • Broadening compressed everything. Assassination dropped 83k → 67k and the field bunched from ~65k down to ~52k across a dozen specs — top-30% groups execute less cleanly, so priority damage clusters. The order among the 50–58k tier (ranks 5–13) is close enough that a few thousand DPS separates six spots.
  • Still small (badged): Frost DK, Subtlety Rogue, Enhancement, Outlaw, Fury, Shadow — under 10 logs, so treat their exact rank loosely.
  • The cleave floor: BM Hunter funnels the hardest (30.6% focus) but does the least raw priority damage (22.8k); Demonology Warlock and Elemental Shaman round out the bottom — output specs that spread damage rather than funnel it.
