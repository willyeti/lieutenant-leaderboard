How to read this board

  • Arcane Mage and Arms Warrior are the bedrock. They're the only two with big samples (77 and 86 keys) at the top — ~65–68k priority DPS. If you want a spec that reliably melts lieutenants in the current meta, it's these two.
  • Rogues punch above their rarity. All three specs — Assassination (#2), Subtlety (#7), Outlaw (#8) — crack the top 10, but each on only 3–4 logs. Rogues are uncommon in top +15–18 comps, so the numbers are noisy; the *direction* (Rogues are elite single-target funnels) is believable, the exact figure isn't settled.
  • Small samples are marked for a reason. Windwalker (#4), the Rogues, Enhance and Frost DK all sit under 10 logs — a couple of great parses can lift a spec several ranks. Trust the SOLID tier (Arcane, Arms, Havoc, Ret) far more than the SMALL tier.
  • Focus vs output: the top specs cluster at ~22–25% focus — priority damage tracks raw single-target output, not just discipline. Off the board, BM Hunter funnels hardest of anyone (34% focus) but does the least damage, and Balance Druid sits at ~20% — the cleave-lean profiles.
